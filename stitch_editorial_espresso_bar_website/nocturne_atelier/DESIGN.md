## Brand & Style

This design system channels an intimate late-night espresso bar hybridized with an avant-garde editorial broadsheet. The emotional register is nocturnal, cinematic, and tactile—evoking the slow pour of a midnight extraction, heavy cardstock menus, low-wattage tungsten illumination, and aromatic steam.

The core design movement combines **Editorial Luxury** and **Dark Tactile Minimalism**:
- **Cinematic Chiaroscuro:** Deep, warm espresso tones offset by glowing crema text and amber accents rather than clinical true-black/white contrast.
- **Editorial Asymmetry:** Dynamic multi-column split layouts, generous narrative scale shifts, and high-tension juxtapositions between monumental expressive serif headlines and utilitarian monospaced readouts.
- **Physicality & Ephemera:** Digitally recreated hospitality artifacts, such as receipt slips with zigzag tear cuts, rotating circular seal stamps, dotted menu leaders, and brass-inspired active states.

## Colors

The palette draws strictly from roasted beans, crema, cast-iron roasters, and amber bar lamps. Cool blues, purples, and artificial neon hues are strictly prohibited.

### Dark Mode (Default)
- **Canvas Base:** `#15100D` (Deep Espresso) serves as the primary canvas, framing all elevated modules.
- **Surface Raised:** `#201813` (Roasted Walnut) provides contrast for interactive cards, sheets, and popovers.
- **Surface Overlay:** `#2C201A` for hover interactions, active states, and elevated modal panels.
- **Border Subtle:** `#3A2C23` (Charred Rim) for hair-thin structural dividers, cards, and input boundaries.
- **Main Text:** `#F3E6D3` (Crema) provides organic readability without the eye fatigue of pure white.
- **Muted Text:** `#A8978A` (Steamed Ash) for secondary descriptions, metadata, and inactive icons.
- **Primary Accent:** `#E2572B` (Burnt Orange) delivers focused focal energy for primary call-to-actions, vital alerts, and active cart badges.
- **Secondary Accent:** `#E8A33D` (Saffron Glow) used exclusively for ratings, origin tags, roasting profiles, and delicate indicators.

### Inverted Editorial Print Mode (High-Contrast Light Sections)
Used selectively for feature broadsheets, receipt drawouts, and editorial long-form articles:
- **Canvas:** `#F3E6D3` (Crema Paper)
- **Ink Primary:** `#15100D` (Espresso)
- **Ink Muted:** `#6C5B51` (Ground Husk)
- **Ink Border:** `#D9C7B0`

## Typography

Typography functions as the primary visual texture. The typographic voice uses three distinct families:
1. **Newsreader:** Optical-sized editorial serif for large display and dramatic quotes. Pair Roman regular weights with occasional italic flourishes within the same sentence to emphasize single origin notes, brewing rituals, or bar quotes.
2. **Hanken Grotesk:** Crisp, understated grotesque sans-serif for comfortable reading, form elements, descriptions, and functional UI navigation.
3. **Space Mono:** Raw, mechanical monospaced numbers and tags reminiscent of receipt printers, roaster serial stamps, timestamp tickers, tasting metric notes, and pricing tables.

Text tracking should tighten proportionally on large editorial headlines and expand gracefully on monospaced metadata and small-caps badge tokens.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout model is anchored by a structured 12-column asymmetric grid capable of breaking into dynamic bento boxes and two-column editorial split sheets.

### Breakpoints & Adaptive Layout
- **Mobile (< 768px):** 4-column layout. Margin of `1.25rem` (`margin`), gutter of `1.5rem`. Split layouts collapse into stacked modules. Sticky compact order dock at screen base.
- **Tablet (768px - 1024px):** 8-column layout. Margin of `2.5rem` (`margin-tablet`), gutter of `1.5rem`. Asymmetric pairings (e.g., 5-col image, 3-col tasting notes).
- **Desktop (1024px+):** 12-column layout. Margin of `5rem` (`margin-desktop`), gutter of `2.5rem` (`gutter-desktop`). Maximum content width capped at `1440px` with fluid edge buffers.

### Layout Motifs
- **Bento Composition:** Irregular modular tiles with solid `#201813` fills bound together with 1px `#3A2C23` borders.
- **Editorial Dotted Leaders:** Horizontal dashed or dotted hairline paths bridging article item names directly to right-aligned monospace prices.
- **Full-Bleed Bleed-Throughs:** High-contrast paper sections (`#F3E6D3`) expand full width between dark sections, creating a physical magazine insert cadence.

## Elevation & Depth

This design system avoids generic multi-tier blur drop-shadows. Depth is articulated purely through surface tiering, low-contrast hairline borders, and warm atmospheric glows.

### Elevation Hierarchy
1. **Tier 0 (Base Canvas):** `#15100D` flat plane. Zero shadow.
2. **Tier 1 (Resting Cards & Surfaces):** `#201813` surface, delineated by a 1px border of `#3A2C23`.
3. **Tier 2 (Interactive Hover / Floating Nav / Drawers):** `#2C201A` surface with an ambient atmospheric amber halo:
   - `box-shadow: 0 16px 36px -12px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.65), 0 0 1px 1px rgba(243, 230, 211, 0.08)`
4. **Tier 3 (Active Overlays / Reservation Modals):** Backdrop blur of `16px` with a tinted overlay (`rgba(21, 16, 13, 0.82)`) and edge highlight `border: 1px solid rgba(243, 230, 211, 0.15)`.

### Lighting Direction
Simulate a single high-angle low-wattage point source: cards gain an internal top highlight via `box-shadow: inset 0 1px 0 0 rgba(243, 230, 211, 0.06)`.

## Shapes

The shape system pairs generous component radii with tailored architectural cuts:
- **Cards, Images & Bento Containers:** Fixed `16px` border radius (`rounded-lg`), framing photography and dark surfaces like polished glass tiles.
- **Buttons, Pill Tags & Interactive Badges:** Fully rounded capsule/pill contours (`border-radius: 9999px`).
- **Inputs & Form Controls:** `12px` roundedness for subtle comfort without clashing against card edges.
- **Receipt Cards:** Top corners rounded to `16px`; bottom edge constructed with CSS mask/SVG repeating triangular zig-zag sawtooth serrations (`8px` pitch) mimicking thermal receipt paper tear-offs.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary:** Pill-shaped (`rounded-full`), solid Burnt Orange `#E2572B` background, Crema `#F3E6D3` bold text (`Hanken Grotesk`, `14px`, 600 weight). Hover state transitions to `#C9461D` with subtle scale-up (`scale(1.02)`).
- **Secondary / Ghost:** Pill-shaped, transparent background, 1px border of `#3A2C23`, Crema `#F3E6D3` text. Hover state shifts border to `#E8A33D` and surface to `rgba(232, 163, 61, 0.06)`.
- **Text Action:** Monospace uppercase link (`Space Mono`, 12px) paired with an animated inline directional arrow (`→`) moving 4px right on hover.

### Chips & Badges
- **Status & Origin Chips:** Height `28px`, pill-shaped, background `rgba(232, 163, 61, 0.12)`, border 1px solid `rgba(232, 163, 61, 0.3)`, text `#E8A33D` in `Space Mono` 11px uppercase.
- **Rotating Circular Stamp Badge:** A self-contained circular SVG (diameter `96px` to `120px`) with animated continuous clockwise CSS rotation (20s linear). Monospace text along a curved path ("FRESHLY ROASTED · BREWED WITH LOVE · NOCTURNE ATELIER ·") encircling a static center glyph (minimalist coffee flower or flame icon).

### Menu Lists & Dotted Leaders
- Menu items sit in flexible rows: Left side contains item name (`Newsreader` italic, 20px) and roast profile (`Hanken Grotesk`, 13px muted).
- Center contains a fluid dotted leader line with `border-bottom: 2px dotted #3A2C23` spanning the empty void.
- Right side terminates in the price formatted via `Space Mono` (`#F3E6D3`, 16px).

### Cards & Bento Modules
- **Editorial Cards:** Background `#201813`, 16px radius, 1px border `#3A2C23`. Images inside cards feature subtle zoom transitions (`transform: scale(1.04)` over 600ms cubic-bezier).
- **Thermal Receipt Card:** Inverted paper background (`#F3E6D3`), ink text (`#15100D`). Typography strictly `Space Mono`. Includes mock barcode, dashed separator cuts, timestamp metadata, table assignment, and serrated bottom edge.

### Inputs & Selectors
- **Input Fields:** Dark surface `#18120F`, border 1px solid `#3A2C23`, text `#F3E6D3`, placeholder `#6C5B51`. Focus ring glows with an amber accent `box-shadow: 0 0 0 1px #E8A33D`.
- **Checkboxes & Radios:** Custom 18px rounded squares (checkbox) or circles (radio), border 1.5px `#3A2C23`. Active state filled with Burnt Orange `#E2572B` and Crema check indicator.

### Floor Plan Seat Selector
- Architectural SVG plan rendered in `#201813` on `#15100D` canvas.
- Tables framed in `#3A2C23`. Available seats rendered as unfilled capsules with `#A8978A` borders; reserved seats filled with muted `#2C201A`; selected seat pulses with Burnt Orange `#E2572B` fill and saffron glow.
