Colophon & Origins

Born at 2:00 AM

Nocturne Atelier was founded inside a quiet basement laboratory in Indiranagar by three nocturnal thinkers: a typographer, an acoustic engineer, and a Q-grader. Frustrated by coffee houses that closed their heavy shutters just as our focus sharpened, we built a nocturnal sanctuary.

Every extraction here honors the night dweller. We roast on a restored 1978 San Franciscan drum roaster, profiling each bean to preserve origin acidity and complex florals without the astringent punch of commercial caffeine. We pair this with curated vinyl pressings played through hand-wound vacuum tube amplifiers.
